Why are fossils on different continents?

Why are the same fossils on different continents?

Fossils can be found on different continents because at some point all the continents were Together in a large land mass known as Pangea, And animals Would have been able to cross the landmass easily, and died And then fossilised, if they were in the right conditions.

Plants or ferns can also spread without the need of a landmass

Ie. a fern, with seeds that float in the air, which allows the seed to travel continents
